title-first Director+ labels, explicit non-recruiting provenance, title-primary role
matching, complete record hashes and mechanically enforced runtime/audit gates.

- Cutoff: 2026-04-01
- Validation window: [2026-04-01, 2026-07-01)
- Candidate universe: 15 previously unused companies, five per company type
- Prediction inputs: A-grade non-recruiting public evidence only; no JOSINT, jobs,
  workforce clusters or validation-window labels
