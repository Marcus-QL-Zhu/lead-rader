# Uniform future-job label audit

A separate agent, denied access to the prediction snapshot, searched all nine
frozen candidates for jobs published in `[2026-04-01, 2026-07-01)`. The same
inclusion rule was used throughout: explicit Director, substantive
Director-level functional Head, VP or CxO; exact publication date; correct
company or China legal entity/scope. Managers, experts, engineers, assistants,
individual contributors, relative dates and wrong group entities were
excluded.

The common query template combined each Chinese/English company name with
招聘/Jobs, 总监/Director/Head/VP/负责人 and the three observation months, then
cross-checked JobUI, BOSS/Liepin, LinkedIn and official careers/ATS pages.
